# Temply brand v2 close-out — 7 October 2026

Brings the Temply Brand Pack (a Claude canvas artifact, `claude.ai/artifact/FRcH5hvTLYk8AQELfAkwvv`) into the product, planned in `.claude/.planning/v2/` (six steps; the running record is `v2/Current.md`). Steps 1 to 5 are built and committed locally, not pushed. Step 6 is partly done: the gates, the sweep greps and a pass against the brand guideline are done, and the independent review and the signed-in browser pass are not (see [Not done](#not-done)).

Nothing signed-in has been seen working. The sidebar lockup, the mobile bar, the sign-in screen and the loader inside a real route are verified by unit tests, the public specs and standalone renders only, until CI's signed-in run or you open them.

## What changed, by step

1. **Tokens and `BrandMark`.** A static `--brand-*` block (coral, pink, purple, violet, navy and the gradient) in `globals.css`, the same in light and dark and never read by the app UI. `BrandMark` is the pack's single path with a per-instance gradient id and a `mono` variant. `brand-tokens.test.ts` fails if a brand colour is read outside an allow-list.
2. **Icons and manifest.** The satori icon routes and `mark.tsx` are gone. `app/icon0.svg`, `icon1.png`, `apple-icon.png` and six files in `public/brand/` are the pack's gradient icon. The manifest points at them. `brand-colour.test.ts` was rewritten (5 tests to 14; step 3 added 4 more) and `brand.e2e.ts` is new.
3. **The lockup in the app.** `BrandLogo` (two `<img>`s, the `.dark` class picks the file) replaces the live-text "Temply" in the sidebar, the dashboard mobile bar, the sign-in layout and the marketing header. The other `BrandMark` sites went gradient. In forced colours the file follows the system's scheme.
4. **Social card and SEO.** The pack's 1200 by 630 card (93 KB) at `/brand/temply-social-card-1200x630.png`; `/og-image.png` and `/temply-email-editor.png` 301 to it; the structured-data logo is the 512 gradient icon; the old card is deleted.
5. **The logo in new emails.** `/brand/temply-mark-email.png`, a 144 by 144 square (6 KB) with the 9:8 mark centred on transparent bands, because the Logo block gives an image one number for width and height. Used by the starters, the default document and the header and footer blocks through `EMAIL_MARK_SRC`. `brand/mark.png` and `brand/logo.png` are untouched and their hashes pinned.
6. **Sweep (so far).** Three misses against the guideline, fixed: the page loader still drew the retired three-bar tile (now the gradient app icon with the mark's own three shapes, `MARK_PARTS`); the share page's header mark was 14px, under the 24px minimum; the inline-image placeholder was the violet `mark.png` at 20px (now the 32px app icon, `EMAIL_ICON_SRC`). `brand-size.test.ts` now fails any `BrandMark` or `BrandLogo` under 24px.

## Decisions taken

All on their defaults in [decisions.md](../.planning/v2/decisions.md), on your word to do the recommended by your brand guideline: A1 to A3 (the app keeps its violet, its type and its neutrals; brand colour is for the mark and artwork), B1 (the wordmark is an image), B2 (the in-app mark is the gradient), B3 (a small static token set), B4 (`theme_color` stays `#5B45E0`), B5 (the social image has a new URL, with 301s), B6 (11 of the pack's 60 files ship), B7 (the image alt follows the card), B8 (`mark.png` and `logo.png` are never overwritten; new emails link a new file) and B9 (below).

Calls made inside the steps, each in `Current.md`: the header lockup is 30px (24px on a phone), not the board's 35, because 35 does not fit at `md`; no `tone` prop, because the rail follows the theme; the email mark is a 144px square with transparent bands, not the mark's own shape; the 300 KB limit on the card and the `og:image:alt` are tests; the loader is the icon tile and not the bare mark, so it reads on any painted canvas.

## Sweep backlog

- **Clear space.** The guideline asks for about a third of the mark's height clear on every side. Checked only in the welcome starter; not in the other starters or in the header and footer blocks, where the mark sits beside text.
- **Marketing artwork at 1280 and 390.** No hand-drawn mark by grep; not looked at.
- **The iOS home-screen icon.** The pack's 180px PNG has transparent corners, which iOS paints black. The old icon had them too. An opaque full-bleed file from the pack would fix it.
- **`temply-app-icon-gradient-16.png`** is shipped, but no code or markup references it; `brand-colour.test.ts` only checks its size.
- **A rollback gap.** The new email mark ships in the same deploy as the content that names it, so an Instant Rollback leaves a template created after the deploy pointing at a file the older release lacks. Two deploys (the file, then the content) would close it. Not done; the window is small.
- **Templates made before v2** keep the violet logo, by B8, and so does all mail already delivered. `brand/mark.png` and `brand/logo.png` can go once the 30-day retention for pinned versions has passed (remove their entries from `email-mark.test.ts` in the same change).
