# Temply redesign close-out — 7 October 2026

Closes the redesign planned in `.claude/.planning/` (steps 1 to 10). The work is complete and committed locally in eight layers (see [Commits](#commits)), not pushed. Before committing it was 110 tracked files changed (+6121, −2573), 4 deleted, 66 new, plus the brand and workspace-switcher changes made afterwards. This pass ran step 10: gates on the whole tree, a sweep of the surfaces nobody had restyled, a six-layer review of the diff, fixes, a browser check of what can run here, then the brand colour, the switcher's CI pictures and the commits.

Nothing signed-in has been seen working. Every dashboard, template and editor surface is verified by unit tests and the gates only, until CI's signed-in run or you open it. See [What was seen](#what-was-seen).

## What changed, by step

Step numbers follow the pasted plan, not the planner's.

1. **Tokens.** Violet accent `#5B45E0` (`--ds-accent`), `--ds-focus` and the 3px focus ring, washes, `--ds-track` and `--ds-disabled` for disabled controls, `shadow-cta`. Radii run `xs` 4 to `2xl` 20, plus `rounded-field` 14, `rounded-card` 24 and `rounded-panel` 32. The shadow ladder is `xs` chip or thumbnail, `sm` card at rest, `md` popover, `lg` menu, `xl` dialog or drawer. Friendlier fonts landed first (Figtree, Bricolage Grotesque, JetBrains Mono).
2. **Primitives.** `Button` sizes `sm` h-7, `compact` h-8, `md` h-12 (default), `lg` h-14, `icon`, `icon-sm`; `pressable` and the new `ariaDisabled` are exported. `Input` exports `fieldClass`, and `Textarea` is new on the same tokens. A disabled control is a filled track pill or field in the disabled ink, never the live control at half strength. `aria-disabled` is used where the control must keep keyboard focus. Card, Badge and segmented control moved with them. `bare-button.test.ts` fails any `<Button>` without an explicit `size`.
3. **Shell.** 248px sidebar, 44px nav rows, trial card, user menu, mobile nav, the Clerk workspace switcher in `sidebar.tsx`, its trigger pinned in `globals.css`.
4. **Templates list.** Stage pills, thumbnails, row menu, search.
5. **Dashboard home.** Header, starter chips, next-step banner, recent templates, four usage tiles (API calls, Templates, Storage, Members). `GetStarted` is deleted. Date helpers are `lib/stamp.ts` (`parseStamp` for zone-less API timestamps) and `lib/relative-time.ts`.
6. **Editor frame.** Header, tab row, canvas card, `editor-actions`, `editor-view-switch`, `save-status`, `template-frame`.
7. **Editor rails.** Components rail and Email settings rail on a shared `rail-frame`, each collapsing to 72px and remembering it per rail in `localStorage`. The landmark "Add content" is now "Components".
8. and 9a. **Marketing header, footer, hero, workflow.** Shared `site-footer`, `container`, `workflow-card`.
9b. **Pricing band and closing band.** `seat-stepper` and `seat-sum`, with every figure from `shared/plans.ts`. The Free trial card stays.
10. **Sweep and review** (this pass), below.
11. **Brand colour (F2, resolved).** The mark's tile, `app/icon0.svg`, `public/brand/icon.svg`, the manifest theme colour, the `global-error.tsx` button and the social card moved from cobalt `#0B5FFF` to the accent `#5B45E0`. `mark.png` and `logo.png` are re-rendered from the SVG at 512×512 with the same silhouette. Satori, the manifest and the error page cannot read a CSS variable, so they repeat the fill, and `scripts/brand-colour.test.ts` fails when any of them drifts from `--ds-accent`. Two side effects: starter email templates reference `/brand/mark.png` by URL, so their logos now render violet, and the social card's neutrals are still the earlier slate.
12. **Workspace switcher pictures.** `e2e/specs/workspace-switcher.e2e.ts` asserts the pinned trigger geometry in both themes and attaches four pictures (closed and open, light and dark); a CI step uploads them as the `workspace-switcher` artifact whether the run passes or not. It is a file of its own because a 1.5px border only computes at a real device density of 2, which is a worker-level launch option. It skips the phone project (emulated density 3 draws 1.33px). Artifacts on this repo are public, so only the named pictures go up; they show the e2e workspace's name.

### Step 10 changes

- **Fields.** Hand-rolled inputs, selects and textareas on api-keys, brands, assets, the asset picker, template versions, connect-app, the mobile editor sheets, the contact form, invite-teammates and template review now use `Input`, `Textarea` or `fieldClass`.
- **Disabled.** The contact form's Sending button, the pricing seat stepper's bound, the locked live/test key radio and disabled dropdown-menu items now use the track pill and disabled ink instead of `opacity-*`. `ariaDisabled` carries the button case.
- **Motion.** `scroll-to-top` transitions opacity and transform on the duration and ease tokens, with `motion-reduce:transition-none`.
- **Dead code.** `Kicker.onAccent` removed. `--ds-rail-line` and `--color-rail-line` removed from `globals.css`; the redesign dropped the border they drew. `get-started.tsx`, `locale-date.ts` (and its test) and `showcase-row.tsx` are deleted.
- **Tidy.** Seven multi-line `<Button size="compact">` tags reformatted; trailing blank lines removed so `git diff --check HEAD` is clean.
- **Specs added.** `marketing.e2e.ts`: the stepper at its bound paints the filled disabled pill at full opacity, and the contact form paints its send button as the disabled pill while it sends. `redesign-primitives.e2e.ts`: a text box wears the field's border and corners on a taller box. Unit tests: `textarea.test.tsx` (3), a `ariaDisabled` case in `button.test.tsx`, and a Sending-button case in `contact-form.test.tsx`.

Each needs a redesign or a browser, not a token fix.

- **Dialog, popover and menu geometry.** They keep the old 12 and 10px radii and a 1px border, where the board draws none. A field at 14px now sits inside a 12px dialog.
- **Clerk `cardBox` appearances** (sign-in, sign-up, create workspace, settings) need a browser to judge.

- **The mobile editor layout and its sheets** were not in the plan.
- **Unused tokens that predate the redesign:** `--ds-rail-raised`, `--ds-rail-hover`, and `--ds-rail-active-bg` and `-ink` (the last two are guarded in `check:contrast` for a selected rail row that does not exist).
